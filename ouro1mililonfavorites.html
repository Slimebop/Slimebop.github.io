OuroboroSalvation.

The shadow of the progenitors is long, ever-growing, but our Heroes and their Squires still chase the dwindling light of tomorrow. Humans, Fiends, Sirens, Trolls, and more are caught up in the spiral of SBURB, forced to partake in the world-ending game(now out of beta!) while navigating the increasingly complex new world of growing up.

Despair and hope, gloom and warmth. This is not an easy tale, for it strikes at the hearts of the broken, the mending, and the lost. Yet, together in the dark, the light shines brighter.

Flee with us.

Act 1, Act 2, and the start of Act 3 have been remade while the rest of Act 3 and several panels later on are due for remaking. Act 4 and beyond are my new standard of quality. Remake progress found here.

Newcomer artist:
The art is lackluster at first, but it steadily improves as I figure out art and regain control over my motor functions (I have difficulty doing most technical and precise work thanks to disability and brain damage as a child). I am remaking the older panels steadily while making the new panels, but I am still leaving links to the old panels because part of the reason I made this adventure was to have a solid visual timeline of my growth. I plan to work on this for years to come.

If you really can't bear to look at certain art styles/errors, then here are some milestones of art progress. You can always go back to read the older stuff if you like the other adventure aspects! Also, I would say every page before 875 is in a state of semi-canon. I say this because they are subject to change to be a better fit with my view. So don't be too shocked when someone or someplace looks different suddenly down the line.
512:Anti-aliasing is mostly turned off. It fully turns off over time.
703:Image size consistency(mostly).
875:Smaller sprites.

Triggering Content:
Now, OSAL is a bit blunt in its topics. But rest assured, all things serve a purpose. By the nature of the comic, there are bound to be sensitive topics. However, it will NOT be glorifying most of it. This comic is not made for these topics, but the topics will arise when fitting a character(s), scene(s), or event(s). Additionally, this comic is not made to antagonize any group(s) of people and does not claim to be responsible for directly offending you. The comic simply is what it is, and what it is, is a GRIM story of harsh realities. Because only in darkness can light shine.

Despite the gloom and harshness, there will be HOPE. The comic wishes that people will connect with the characters with their shared trauma and grow alongside them to overcome it. Homestuck helped Inknautilus out of a dark spot in their life, and now they want to help others just as it did.

Here is a general list of potentially triggering content. A more detailed list is being created.
-Flashing lights
-Violence, death, and crime
-Abuse and neglect
-Manipulation, gaslighting, and peer pressure
-Suicide and mental anguish
-Sexual themes, innuendos, direct sexual references(no uncensored nudity)
-SERIOUS sexual trauma and anguish
-Explicit language
-Discrimination and body shaming
-Substance use and abuse
-Existential and religious themes
-Disturbing visuals (e.g., spiders, scars, blood)
-More to come

If you find one that isn't listed and can give a good argument for why it should be added to the list, then message me! :D
